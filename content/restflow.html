Why Restflow

One connected flow, not separate tools

Purchases feed inventory. Stock moves to the kitchen for production. Recipes link production to menu items, and POS sales show what was expected to be used. Variance and waste dashboards then show where reality differs, and analytics dashboards bring it together.

Inventory

Inventory management and monitoring, stock movement, purchases, utilisation.

Kitchen and product

Kitchen production, recipe formulation, menu development.

Sales

Overall business and inventory monitoring dashboards.
