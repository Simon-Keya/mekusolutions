One operational picture, across the working day

Genuine screens from the product. Each view shows a different part of restaurant operations, from management oversight to stock control and order handling.

Management

See the operation at a glance

The dashboard brings revenue, order activity, stock alerts and pending approvals into one management view.

Inventory

Know what is in stock, and what needs attention

Inventory screens show tracked items, stock value, reorder warnings, batch expiry and item-level availability.

Batch control

Follow stock at batch level

Expanded item details expose individual batches, received dates, expiry dates, remaining quantities and status.

Point of sale

Connect menu availability to order activity

The POS view displays orders by status alongside menu availability indicators, including items affected by low stock.

Order tracking

Review recent orders and their status

Recent order records show order reference, service type, item count, total, fulfilment status, payment status and time placed.

Stock variance

Compare counted stock with expected quantities

The variance view records stock-count activity and surfaces flagged items and variance values for review.
