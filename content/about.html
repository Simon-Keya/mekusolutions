About Meku

We started Meku to make business operations more visible.

Meku Solutions was founded in October 2025 to help businesses understand how their capital is used, by connecting the processes that produce their data. We start with inventory-intensive businesses, beginning with restaurants through Restflow.

Our approach

Systems are designed with people who do the operational work, around how the process really happens.

Vision

To be the leading business system connector and visibility software company in Africa.

Mission

Build efficient channels for businesses that deal with inventory through technology, bringing clarity and data-driven decision-making to companies.

Team
