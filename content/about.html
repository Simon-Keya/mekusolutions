About

We started Meku to make business operations more visible

Our story

Meku Solutions was founded in October 2025 with the ambition of helping businesses understand how their capital is being utilised by connecting the operational processes that produce their data.

Meku believes businesses should not have to work with disconnected information to make important decisions.

The long-term vision is to become a leading business-system connector and visibility software company in Africa. The journey begins with inventory-intensive businesses, starting with restaurants through Restflow.

Vision

To be the leading business system connector and visibility software company in Africa.

Mission

Build efficient channels for businesses that deal with inventory through technology, bringing clarity and data-driven decision-making to companies.

Why Meku was started

To create visibility of capital utilisation in business and create employment opportunities.

Approach

Built from the ground perspective

Systems are designed with people who actually perform the operational work. The question is not simply “What features can software have?” but “How does this process actually happen, and how can technology make it clearer and more effective?”
