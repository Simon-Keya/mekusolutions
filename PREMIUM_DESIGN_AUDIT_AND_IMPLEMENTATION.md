# Meku Solutions — Premium Design Audit & Implementation

## Source of truth
This pass is based on `Meku-Solutions-Premium-Website-Refined.zip`, supplied by the user. It deliberately preserves the orange/yellow/black Meku identity, logo, favicon, existing Next.js routes, Restflow content, Trace concept, enquiry flow, and six genuine Restflow screenshots.

## Reference-led design principles applied
- **Zain's Studio / HR Management hero:** direct value proposition, clear hierarchy, concise supporting explanation and a prominent action. The dark/green palette was not copied.
- **Sellora dashboard UI:** product interfaces should be framed cleanly and presented as credible evidence. Meku uses its own real Restflow screenshots rather than a fabricated dashboard.
- **Navarino by KOTA:** simplify complex technology through strong typography, restrained micro-interactions, clear navigation and visual explanations.
- **NewBase corporate template:** use confident corporate structure, restrained gradients and a deliberate balance of services, credibility, company story and contact actions.
- **Muze B2B website article:** explain actual expertise and operational problems instead of relying on generic claims. Preserve factual statements and avoid invented metrics, testimonials or outcomes.

## Implemented in this pass
- Increased typographic confidence and body-text readability while preserving the existing font families and warm brand palette.
- Strengthened the hero hierarchy and made the genuine Restflow dashboard preview the primary visual proof.
- Refined the Restflow gallery into larger alternating editorial compositions with more space for each explanation.
- Gave the Trace concept stronger visual prominence as a distinctive interactive product story.
- Changed service rows into a more deliberate editorial index with clearer hierarchy and responsive layout.
- Improved the problem/approach narrative with stronger section structure and numbered editorial rhythm.
- Refined orange/yellow use as purposeful accents rather than applying colour indiscriminately.
- Kept motion restrained and included responsive and reduced-motion behaviour.

## Explicitly preserved
- Original Meku logo, favicon, orange/yellow/black palette and brand identity.
- Existing page routes, content structure, navigation and calls to action.
- Restflow Trace JavaScript, ingredient definitions and interaction logic.
- Enquiry form, validation, API handling and analytics implementation.
- Original six Restflow screenshot files; no fabricated product screens, no artificial sharpening and no replacement imagery.
- Product claims, pilot status and factual caveats; no invented customer results or integration status.
